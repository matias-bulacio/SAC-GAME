The player takes on the role of the astronauts stranded in Arcadia Planitia, in the northern hemisphere of Mars.

Considered one of the most suitable locations for human exploration due to its accessible subsurface water ice (NASA, April 2021), Arcadia Planitia is home to the Martian outpost.

It was SOL 521 since the crew's landing on Mars. A critical, scheduled software update was underway. Everything was running smoothly. The outpost was fully operational, and the mission remained on schedule.

Until alarms blared and red warning lights flooded the habitat.

A solar storm of considerable magnitude had erupted. Initial forecasts suggested it would be little more than a minor disturbance. A subsequent assessment seemed to confirm that it would not last much longer. Back on Earth, Mission Control faced a critical decision: suspend data transmission and abort the software update, or proceed with the update while addressing the electrical issues it was causing.

A heated argument broke out between the Systems Engineers and the Flight Director. Ultimately, the second option was approved.

The consequences were catastrophic.

The storm lasted two hours longer than initially predicted, eventually damaging the primary communications system. The astronauts attempted to activate the system recovery protocol, but the storm had corrupted the entire recovery system.

No communications.

Stranded millions of kilometers from Earth, the crew was now on its own.

The repair parts were sended a month later, it will land on SOL 630.
