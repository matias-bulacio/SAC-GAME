\section{Decisions}

\indent

The player will have to decide between different events that occur or decisions that will define the future of the crew. Such as decide between planting potatoes or sweet potatoes, or how they will face a solar storm. 



\subsection{Card System}

 The player can encounter 4 types of situations: 

 \begin{itemize}
    
    \item Creations: Optional decisions that allow the player to build or install new structures, such as additional solar panels.
    \item Problems: Unavoidable events that have a negative consecuence, which may or may not be mitigated.
    \item Actions: Activities the player can perform at any time to improve certain aspects of the outpost. For example, supercharging the radiation shield to increase its protection level, at the cost of higher power consumption.
    \item Incidents: Activities the player can perform at any time to improve certain aspects of the outpost. For example, overcharging the radiation shield to increase its protection level, at the cost of higher power consumption.

 \end{itemize}
 
For each situation, the player must choose between two possible options. Each choice affects the outpost's resource indicators, and the game also reports whether an incident has occurred.
